# Turn every invite into a competition.

### Launch a referral event in minutes. Watch the leaderboard fill in real time.

You're growing a WhatsApp group, a launch waitlist, or a community — and
your best growth channel is the people already in it. Referral App gives
every one of them a personal link, a live leaderboard spot, and a countdown
that makes sharing feel like a game instead of a favor.

---

## How it works

**1. Create your event.**
Give it a name, a target group link, a duration, and an optional cover
image for link previews. We handle the rest.

**2. Your people register.**
Anyone joining gets their own short, shareable link and a ready-to-send
invite message — no copywriting required.

**3. They share. You watch the board move.**
Every real click counts. Every duplicate click, bot preview, or repeat tap
from the same device is filtered out automatically — so the leaderboard
always reflects real reach, not noise.

**4. The event closes, the winners are clear.**
A live countdown keeps urgency high until the very end, and your dashboard
shows exactly who drove the most real referrals.

---

## Built for the moment it actually goes viral

A referral link doesn't get a warm-up lap — it either gets shared into a
group of five people or a group of five thousand, and you find out which
one *after* it happens. Referral App's redirect engine is built to handle
that spike without slowing down, without double-counting a click, and
without ever mistaking a WhatsApp link preview for a real visit.

- **Instant redirects.** No loading spinner between someone tapping a link
  and landing in your group.
- **Fair scoring, automatically.** The same person clicking their own link
  five times doesn't move them up the board — real, distinct clicks do.
- **Clean previews everywhere.** Share a link in WhatsApp or Telegram and
  it unfurls into a proper card — your event name, your image, your
  description — without that preview fetch ever touching the scoreboard.

---

## For the people sharing

Every participant gets their own lightweight dashboard: current score,
current rank, time left on the clock, and a **one-tap button** that copies
their link *and* a ready-to-post write-up together. No friction between
"I want to share this" and actually doing it.

---

## For you, running it

A live dashboard shows total referrals, active participants, and your
duplicate/fraud-blocked rate at a glance — plus a full, searchable,
filterable log of every click, exportable to CSV whenever you need the raw
data.
